How to Install on Nintendo 3DS
SD Card Destination Folder: /opticraft/.minecraft/skins/
  1. Connect your 3DS SD card to your computer or phone (via card reader or FTPD).
  2. Copy the downloaded .png file directly into: /opticraft/.minecraft/skins/ (or /opticraft/skins/).
  3. Open OptiCraft on your 3DS, head to Options > Skins, select your skin, and enjoy!
